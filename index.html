Growing food where the climate is under control.

Meritocratia Life Techs develops controlled-environment cultivation systems and the plant nutrition that goes with them — built from a decade of hands-on work in hydroponics, aeroponics and microbial control.

Work

Three things we do

01

Cultivation systems

Growing systems for protected environments: media design, irrigation, root-zone climate and structural framing. Developed and tested in our own facility.

02

Plant nutrition

Media and nutrition formulations, including biological approaches to keeping the root zone healthy rather than sterilising it.

03

Production

Small-scale, high-value crops grown under cover and sold close to home — starting with microgreens.

Approach

Measure the whole system, not the parts

A plant, a medium and the microbial community in it form one system. None of them behaves the same alone, and a number taken from one of them tells you very little about the other two.

We build systems we can actually observe: where the root zone can be sampled, where a change in nutrition shows up as a measurable difference, and where a bad result is a result.

Background

Biochemistry, applied

The company is founded on a background in biochemistry and molecular biology from the University of Copenhagen, followed by independent development of hydroponic and aeroponic systems from 2016 onward.

That work has run through bamboo-framed growing towers, chamomile-based media trials, and a sunken greenhouse in Frederiksværk — all of it built by hand before it was written down.
